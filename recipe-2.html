Caraway Seed Cake

From Ryzon Baking Book

❧ ───── ❦ ───── ❧

A traditional seed cake perfumed with caraway and orange.

Historical Context

Ryzon Caraway Seed Cake

Ingredients

  • ½ cup butter
  • ½ cup sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 4 tablespoons milk
  • 1 teaspoon orange extract
  • 2½ teaspoons caraway seeds
  • 2 teaspoons Ryzon baking powder
  • 1½ cups flour

Method

  1. Cream butter and sugar.
  2. Beat in eggs, then milk, orange extract and caraway seeds.
  3. Fold in flour sifted with the original Ryzon leavening.
  4. Transfer to a buttered and floured small square tin; bake in a moderate oven for about 40 minutes.
Original source: Ryzon Baking Book (c. 1920), Ryzon Baking Powder Company. Read the digitized cookbook

Fried Drop Cakes

The Boston Cooking-School Cook Book

❧ ───── ❦ ───── ❧

Historical Recipe

The following text is extracted from the 1910 edition, retaining historical terminology and quantities.

Original Ingredients & Directions

1⅓ cups flour

2½ teaspoons baking powder

¼ teaspoon salt

⅓ cup sugar

½ cup milk

1 egg

1 teaspoon melted butter

Beat egg until light; add milk, dry ingredients mixed and sifted, and melted butter. Drop by spoonfuls in hot, new, deep fat; fry until light brown and cooked through, which must at first be determined by piercing with a skewer, or breaking apart. Remove with a skimmer, and drain on brown paper.

Original source: Fannie Merritt Farmer, The Boston Cooking-School Cook Book (1910). Read the digitized original.

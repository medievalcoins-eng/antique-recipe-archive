The following text is extracted from the 1910 edition, retaining historical terminology and quantities.

Original Ingredients & Directions

⅓ cup rice

3 tablespoons butter

½ cup canned tomatoes

½ cup cold cooked chicken cut in dice

White Stock highly seasoned

Salt and cayenne

Cook rice in boiling salted water, drain, and pour over hot water to thoroughly rinse. Heat omelet pan, add butter, 90 and as soon as butter is melted add rice. Cook three minutes; then add tomatoes, chicken, and enough stock to moisten. Cook five minutes, and season highly with salt and cayenne. If not rich enough, add more butter.

Original source: Fannie Merritt Farmer, The Boston Cooking-School Cook Book (1910). Read the digitized original.

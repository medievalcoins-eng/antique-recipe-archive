6 lbs. shin of beef

3 quarts cold water

½ teaspoon peppercorns

6 cloves

½ bay leaf

3 sprigs thyme

1 sprig marjoram

2 sprigs parsley

Carrot

½ cup each, cut in dice

Turnip

Onion

Celery

1 tablespoon salt

Wipe beef, and cut the lean meat in inch cubes. Brown one-third of meat in hot frying-pan in marrow from a marrow-bone. Put remaining two-thirds with bone and fat in 114 soup kettle, add water, and let stand for thirty minutes. Place on back of range, add browned meat, and heat gradually to boiling-point. As scum rises it should be removed. Cover, and cook slowly six hours, keeping below boiling-point during cooking. Add vegetables and seasonings, cook one and one-half hours, strain, and cool as quickly as possible.

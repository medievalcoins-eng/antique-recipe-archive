Fruit Punch IV

The Boston Cooking-School Cook Book

❧ ───── ❦ ───── ❧

Historical Recipe

The following text is extracted from the 1910 edition, retaining historical terminology and quantities.

Original Ingredients & Directions

9 oranges 6 lemons 1 cup grated pineapple 1 cup raspberry syrup 1½ cups tea infusion 1¼ cups sugar 1 cup hot water 1 quart Apollinaris

Mix juice of oranges and lemons with pineapple, raspberry syrup, and tea; then add a syrup made by boiling sugar and 44 water fifteen minutes. Turn in punch-bowl over a large piece of ice. Chill thoroughly, and just before serving add Apollinaris.

Original source: Fannie Merritt Farmer, The Boston Cooking-School Cook Book (1910). Read the digitized original.

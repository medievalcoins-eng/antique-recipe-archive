Club Punch

The Boston Cooking-School Cook Book

❧ ───── ❦ ───── ❧

Historical Recipe

The following text is extracted from the 1910 edition, retaining historical terminology and quantities.

Original Ingredients & Directions

1 cup water

2 cups sugar

1 quart Burgundy

1 cup rum

⅓ cup brandy

⅓ cup Benedictine

1 quart Vichy

3 sliced oranges

½ can pineapple

Juice 2 lemons

1 cup tea infusion

Ice.

Make a syrup by boiling water and sugar ten minutes. Mix remaining ingredients, except ice , sweeten to taste with syrup, and pour into punch-bowl over a large piece of ice.

Original source: Fannie Merritt Farmer, The Boston Cooking-School Cook Book (1910). Read the digitized original.

Raw Oysters

The Boston Cooking-School Cook Book

The following text is extracted from the 1910 edition, retaining historical terminology and quantities.

Original Ingredients & Directions

Raw oysters are served on oyster plates, or in a block of ice. Place block of ice on a folded napkin on platter, and 180 garnish the base with parsley and quarters of lemon, or ferns and lemon.

To Block Ice for Oysters. Use a rectangular piece of clear ice, and with hot flatirons melt a cavity large enough to hold the oysters. Pour water from cavity as rapidly as it forms.

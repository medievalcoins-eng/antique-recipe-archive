The following text is extracted from the 1910 edition, retaining historical terminology and quantities.

Original Ingredients & Directions

Yolks 3 eggs

1 egg

½ cup Consommé

⅛ teaspoon salt

Slight grating nutmeg

Few grains cayenne

Beat eggs slightly, add Consommé and seasonings. Pour into a small buttered tin mould, place in pan of hot water, and bake until firm; cool, remove from mould, and cut in fancy shapes.

Original source: Fannie Merritt Farmer, The Boston Cooking-School Cook Book (1910). Read the digitized original.

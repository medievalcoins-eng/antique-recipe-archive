Historical Recipe

The following text is extracted from the 1910 edition, retaining historical terminology and quantities.

Original Ingredients & Directions

1 quart claret wine

½ cup Curaçoa

1 quart Apollinaris

⅓ cup orange juice

2 tablespoons brandy

Sugar

Mint leaves

Cucumber rind

12 strawberries

Mix ingredients, except Apollinaris, using enough sugar to sweeten to taste. Stand on ice to chill, and add chilled Apollinaris just before serving.

Original source: Fannie Merritt Farmer, The Boston Cooking-School Cook Book (1910). Read the digitized original.

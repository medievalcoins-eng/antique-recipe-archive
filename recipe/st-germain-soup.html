St. Germain Soup

The Boston Cooking-School Cook Book

❧ ───── ❦ ───── ❧

Historical Recipe

The following text is extracted from the 1910 edition, retaining historical terminology and quantities.

Original Ingredients & Directions

3 cups White Stock I, II, or III

1 can Marrowfat peas

1 cup cold water

½ onion

Bit of bay leaf

Sprig of parsley

Blade of mace

2 teaspoons sugar

1 teaspoon salt

⅛ teaspoon pepper

2 tablespoons butter

2 tablespoons corn-starch

1 cup milk

Drain and rinse peas, reserving one-third cup; put remainder in cold water with seasonings, and simmer one-half hour; rub through sieve and add stock. Bind with butter and corn-starch cooked together; boil five minutes. Add milk and reserved peas.

Original source: Fannie Merritt Farmer, The Boston Cooking-School Cook Book (1910). Read the digitized original.

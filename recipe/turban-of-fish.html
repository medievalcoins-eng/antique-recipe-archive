Turban of Fish

The Boston Cooking-School Cook Book

❧ ───── ❦ ───── ❧

Historical Recipe

The following text is extracted from the 1910 edition, retaining historical terminology and quantities.

Original Ingredients & Directions

2½ cups cold flaked fish (cod, haddock, halibut, or cusk)

1½ cups milk

1 slice onion

Blade of mace

Sprig of parsley

¼ cup butter

¼ cup flour

½ teaspoon salt

⅛ teaspoon pepper

Lemon juice

Yolks 2 eggs

⅔ cup buttered cracker crumbs

Scald milk with onion, mace, and parsley; remove seasonings. Melt butter, add flour, salt, pepper, and gradually the milk; then add eggs, slightly beaten. Put a layer of fish on 177 buttered dish, sprinkle with salt and pepper, and add a few drops of lemon juice. Cover with sauce, continuing until fish and sauce are used, shaping in pyramid form. Cover with crumbs, and bake in hot oven until crumbs are brown.

Original source: Fannie Merritt Farmer, The Boston Cooking-School Cook Book (1910). Read the digitized original.

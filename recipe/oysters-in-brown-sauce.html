Oysters in Brown Sauce

The Boston Cooking-School Cook Book

❧ ───── ❦ ───── ❧

Historical Recipe

The following text is extracted from the 1910 edition, retaining historical terminology and quantities.

Original Ingredients & Directions

1 pint oysters

¼ cup butter

¼ cup flour

1 cup oyster liquor

½ cup milk

½ teaspoon salt

1 teaspoon Anchovy essence

⅛ teaspoon pepper

Parboil and drain oysters, reserve liquor, heat, strain, and set aside for sauce. Brown butter, add flour, and stir until well browned; then add oyster liquor, milk, seasonings, and oysters. For filling patty cases or vol-au-vents.

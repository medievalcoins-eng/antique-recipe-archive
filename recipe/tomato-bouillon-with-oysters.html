Tomato Bouillon with Oysters

The Boston Cooking-School Cook Book

❧ ───── ❦ ───── ❧

Historical Recipe

The following text is extracted from the 1910 edition, retaining historical terminology and quantities.

Original Ingredients & Directions

1 can tomatoes

1½ quarts bouillon

1 tablespoon chopped onion

½ bay leaf

6 cloves

½ teaspoon celery seed

½ teaspoon peppercorns

1 pint oysters

Mix all ingredients except oysters, and boil twenty minutes. Strain, cool, and clear. Add parboiled oysters, and serve in bouillon cups with small croûtons.

Original source: Fannie Merritt Farmer, The Boston Cooking-School Cook Book (1910). Read the digitized original.

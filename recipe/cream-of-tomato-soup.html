Cream of Tomato Soup

The Boston Cooking-School Cook Book

❧ ───── ❦ ───── ❧

Historical Recipe

The following text is extracted from the 1910 edition, retaining historical terminology and quantities.

Original Ingredients & Directions

½ can tomatoes

2 teaspoons sugar

¼ teaspoon soda

1 quart milk

1 slice onion

4 tablespoons flour

1 teaspoon salt

⅛ teaspoon pepper

⅓ cup butter

Scald milk with onion, remove onion, and thicken milk with flour diluted with cold water until thin enough to pour, being careful that the mixture is free from lumps; cook 141 twenty minutes, stirring constantly at first. Cook tomatoes with sugar fifteen minutes, add soda, and rub through a sieve; combine mixtures, and strain into tureen over butter, salt, and pepper.

Original source: Fannie Merritt Farmer, The Boston Cooking-School Cook Book (1910). Read the digitized original.

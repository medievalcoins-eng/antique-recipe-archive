Historical Recipe

The following text is extracted from the 1910 edition, retaining historical terminology and quantities.

Original Ingredients & Directions

½ cup butter

¾ cup sugar

3 eggs

1½ cups milk

2 cups corn meal

1 cup flour

1 teaspoon salt

5 teaspoons baking powder

½ cup currants

Cream the butter; add sugar, gradually, eggs well beaten, and milk; then add dry ingredients mixed and sifted, and currants. Bake in buttered individual tins.

Original source: Fannie Merritt Farmer, The Boston Cooking-School Cook Book (1910). Read the digitized original.

Veal and Sago Soup

The Boston Cooking-School Cook Book

❧ ───── ❦ ───── ❧

Historical Recipe

The following text is extracted from the 1910 edition, retaining historical terminology and quantities.

Original Ingredients & Directions

2½ lbs. lean veal

3 quarts cold water

¼ lb. pearl sago

2 cups scalded milk

Yolks 4 eggs

Salt and pepper

Order meat from market, very finely chopped. Pick over and remove particles of fat. Cover meat with water, 123 bring slowly to boiling-point, and simmer two hours, skimming occasionally; strain and reheat. Soak sago one-half hour in enough cold water to cover, stir into hot stock, boil thirty minutes, and add milk; then pour mixture slowly on yolks of eggs, slightly beaten. Season with salt and pepper.

Original source: Fannie Merritt Farmer, The Boston Cooking-School Cook Book (1910). Read the digitized original.

Pop-overs

From Better Meals for Less Money

❧ ───── ❦ ───── ❧

Crisp, hollow oven-baked batter rolls served warm.

Historical Context

Recipe 436 — Popovers

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 cup milk
  • ¼ teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon melted butter

Method

  1. Mix the flour and salt.
  2. Add the milk gradually to make a smooth batter.
  3. Beat in the eggs until light, followed by the melted butter.
  4. Beat the batter for two minutes; pour into very hot, buttered iron gem pans.
  5. Bake in a hot oven for 30–35 minutes.
Original source: Better Meals for Less Money (1916), Mary Green. Read the digitized cookbook

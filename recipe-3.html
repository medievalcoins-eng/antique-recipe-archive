Pavini Cake

From Mrs. Beeton's Book of Household Management

❧ ───── ❦ ───── ❧
Historical illustration
in preparation

A substantial Victorian fruit-and-rice cake with raisins, currants, almonds and nutmeg.

Historical Context

Mrs. Beeton, recipe 1771, A Pavini Cake.

Ingredients

  • ½ lb flour
  • ½ lb ground rice
  • ½ lb raisins, stoned and chopped
  • ¼ lb currants
  • ¼ lb butter
  • 2 oz sweet almonds
  • ¼ lb sifted loaf sugar
  • ½ grated nutmeg
  • 1 pint milk
  • 1 teaspoon carbonate of soda

Method

  1. Stone and chop the raisins; wash and dry the currants.
  2. Cream the butter without letting it become oily; blanch and chop the almonds and grate the nutmeg.
  3. Mix the prepared ingredients thoroughly.
  4. Warm the milk and stir in the carbonate of soda; use it to combine the ingredients into a paste.
  5. Butter a cake mould and fill it a little more than halfway; bake in a moderate oven for 1½–2 hours (less if divided into two cakes).
Original source: Mrs. Beeton's Book of Household Management (1861), Isabella Beeton. Read the digitized cookbook

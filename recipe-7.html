Susie's Spider Corn Cake

From The Boston Cooking-School Cook Book

❧ ───── ❦ ───── ❧
Historical illustration
in preparation

An early American skillet-baked corn cake, made with sour milk and baked in a hot oven.

Historical Context

Susie's Spider Corn Cake, corn cake section.

Ingredients

  • 1¼ cups corn meal
  • 2 cups sour milk
  • 1 teaspoon soda
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tablespoons butter

Method

  1. Mix the soda, salt and corn meal.
  2. Gradually add well-beaten eggs and sour milk.
  3. Heat a frying pan and grease its bottom and sides with butter.
  4. Pour in the mixture and bake on the middle rack of a hot oven for 20 minutes.
Original source: The Boston Cooking-School Cook Book (1910), Fannie Merritt Farmer. Read the digitized cookbook

Boston Baked Beans

From Better Meals for Less Money

Slow-baked navy beans with molasses and mustard, a classic New England preparation.

Historical Context

Recipe 247 — Boston Baked Beans

Ingredients

  • 1 quart pea beans
  • ¼ teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 tablespoon salt
  • ¼ cup molasses
  • 1 teaspoon dry mustard
  • ½ lb fat salt pork

Method

  1. Soak beans overnight; drain and simmer in fresh water until tender but intact.
  2. Place in a bean pot with seasonings and scalded, scored salt pork.
  3. Cover with boiling water and bake slowly for eight hours, replenishing water as needed.
  4. Remove the cover for the final hour.
Original source: Better Meals for Less Money (1916), Mary Green. Read the digitized cookbook

Historical recipe paraphrase, not a modern adaptation. Contains pork; the historical version is not vegetarian or vegan. Oven temperature was not specified.

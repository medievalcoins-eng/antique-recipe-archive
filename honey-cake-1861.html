Honey Cake (1855)

From The National Cook Book

❧ ───── ❦ ───── ❧

An American honey cake recorded in 1855, richly flavoured with cinnamon and cream.

Historical Context

Honey Cake, No. 1, recipe 569

Ingredients

  • ¾ lb butter
  • ¾ lb sugar
  • 6 eggs
  • 2 lb flour
  • 1 tablespoon ground cinnamon
  • ½ gill cream
  • 1 quart honey
  • 1 tablespoon dissolved saleratus (historical leavening)

Method

  1. Cream butter and sugar together.
  2. Beat in eggs; incorporate flour, cinnamon, cream and honey.
  3. Beat the mixture thoroughly, then add dissolved saleratus.
  4. Pour into a buttered, paper-lined pan and bake in a slow oven.
Original source: The National Cook Book (1855), A Lady of Philadelphia. Read the digitized cookbook

Faithful paraphrase of the 1855 printed recipe, not a tested modern adaptation. Saleratus is an obsolete leavening agent; do not substitute quantities without a tested conversion.

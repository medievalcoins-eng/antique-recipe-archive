Tea-Cakes

From Mrs. Beeton's Book of Household Management

❧ ───── ❦ ───── ❧
Historical illustration
in preparation

Soft, yeast-raised Victorian tea-cakes, served warm with butter; Mrs. Beeton notes optional currants and sugar.

Historical Context

Mrs. Beeton, recipe 1786, Tea-Cakes.

Ingredients

  • 2 lb flour
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ¼ lb butter or lard
  • 1 egg
  • A walnut-sized piece of German yeast
  • Warm milk, as needed
  • Optional: a few currants and sugar

Method

  1. Mix the dry flour with salt and rub in the butter or lard.
  2. Beat the egg with the yeast; add to the flour with enough warm milk to form a smooth dough.
  3. Knead well and leave in a warm place to rise.
  4. Shape into eight tea-cakes; place on tins and allow to rise briefly again.
  5. Bake in a moderate oven for 15–30 minutes; serve hot with butter.
Original source: Mrs. Beeton's Book of Household Management (1861), Isabella Beeton. Read the digitized cookbook
